# School report-card fidelity reference matrix

The five read-only school sets were rendered at 45 dpi for complete contact sheets and at 120 dpi for representative pages before implementation. The implementation keeps the school identity configurable through `document_branding_version`; it does not copy the supplied school's identity into code.
